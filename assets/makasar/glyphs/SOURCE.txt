Makasar and Lontara letterform images
=====================================

The 49 PNGs here are the letterforms as drawn (black strokes on
transparency), shown at the head of every row of the Makasar page's
reference table:

  mak_*.png   (20)  the Makasar letters, passimbang and end-of-section sign
  lon_*.png   (25)  their Lontara (Bugis) counterparts, pallawa and
                    end-of-section sign
  sign_*.png   (5)  the vowel marks, on a dotted circle — four of the five
                    are the same mark in the same place in both scripts,
                    which is why one set serves both tables

They come from the images in Wikipedia's "Makasar script" article, which
sets the two scripts side by side; on Wikimedia Commons the files are named
`Mak ka.png` / `Lon ka.png` and so on (PNG — there is no vector original).
They were lifted out of a saved copy of that article by the upstream
`makasar` project's `tool/glyphcopy.sh`, which is that project's provenance
record and holds the full Commons-name -> local-name mapping. The vowel-sign
files are its `lon_sign_*` output renamed `sign_*`, both scripts' tables
using them.

Note: the individual Commons licences of these files were NOT verified when
they were ported into cord — check them before redistributing this directory
on its own. The rest of the Makasar feature's bundle is clear: the
`NotoSerifMakasar-Regular.ttf` beside it is under the SIL Open Font License
(see ../OFL.txt).
