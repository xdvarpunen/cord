hanzi — third-party data attribution
====================================

The stroke table in `hanzi_strokes.dart` is generated by the `hanzi` project's
`tool/generate_strokes.mjs` from three independent sources. Their licences
differ, and one of them is share-alike.

The gloss table beside it, `hanzi_glosses.dart`, is generated by this repo's
`tool/hanzi_glosses.mjs` from a fourth — see "Make Me a Hanzi" below, which
covers both the geometry and the readings.

Paths below are as they stand in the upstream `hanzi` project; here both
generated files live in `lib/hanzi/data/`.

animCJK  —  Arphic Public License
----------------------------------

https://github.com/parsimonhi/animCJK
Licence: the Arphic Public License, as published by Arphic Technology Co., Ltd.
https://ftp.gnu.org/non-gnu/chinese-fonts-truetype/LICENSE

Supplies all Korean hanja geometry (`svgsKo`, 535 characters). Itself derived
from the MakeMeAHanzi project and the Arphic PL KaitiM GB / Big5 fonts.

The Arphic Public License requires that redistributions keep the licence notice
and that derived font software remain under the same terms. The Korean glyph

Make Me a Hanzi / hanzi-writer-data
-----------------------------------
Copyright (c) 2016 Shaunak Kishore, and contributors.
https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data

Licence: graphics CC BY 4.0, dictionary CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Supplies all Chinese stroke geometry (outlines and medians). The graphics
derive from the Arphic fonts; see that project's COPYING for the terms those
carry (Arphic Public Licence), and its dictionary data from Unihan and CJKlib.

Also supplies every reading and meaning in `hanzi_glosses.dart`, taken from
that project's `dictionary.txt` by `tool/hanzi_glosses.mjs` in this repo. That
dictionary is CC BY-SA 4.0 — share-alike, and derived in turn from CC-CEDICT
(also CC BY-SA 4.0) and Unihan. The glosses embedded in `hanzi_glosses.dart`,
and any modification of them, must stay under CC BY-SA 4.0 and carry this
attribution. As with KanjiVG above, that obligation attaches to the data, not
to the application code around it.

The romaji for the kana, and the three variant-form aliases (亀→龜, 値→值,
飮→飲), are not from this source — they are written out in the generator.


cnchar / cnchar-order  —  MIT
------------------------------
Copyright (c) 2017-present theajack <theajack@qq.com>.

Supplies Chinese stroke names, fold counts and stroke-order letter codes. Used
at build time only; no cnchar code ships in the application.
